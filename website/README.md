KreaterLink
1. What is the software product?

KreaterLink is a connection app for content creators. It helps creators discover, connect, and collaborate with other creators across platforms like YouTube, Twitch, TikTok, Instagram, and more.

Content creators of all kinds: Twitch, Twitter/X, TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, and more.

5. Potential features

Verified Creator Profiles Showcase your content platforms with verified creator badges and profile details. Example: link your Twitch account and get a verified badge, so other creators know you are the real streamer.

Creator Matching KreaterLink suggests collaborators based on your profile and goals, so you don't have to look yourself. Example: after you list your goals, KreaterLink suggests a streamer in a similar time zone who also wants to co-stream.

Content Discovery Browse a feed of creators to see who is out there. Example: scroll the feed and find a streamer who plays the same games you do.

Direct Messaging Chat with creators and coordinate collaborations through in-app messaging. Example: message a creator you matched with to suggest a co-stream on Saturday night.

Content Filters Narrow a list of creators by platform, content type, game, and collaborative interests. Example: show only YouTube creators who make horror game videos and are open to collabs.

Integrated Calendar Schedule collaborations based on your availability with an integrated calendar. Example: post that you are free Friday from 7 to 11 p.m., and other creators can claim a slot.
