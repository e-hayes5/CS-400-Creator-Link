Interview 2

Date: 09/23/2026

Interviewer: Liz Olin

Format: Virtual

Interview notes

1. How do you feel currently about finding other content creators to collab with?

I find a bit tough to try and find others to collab with. Time differences or sometimes they are busy

2. What problem would you like to solve as related to collaborating?

Get better at being more active in a community that I would like to collab in.

3. Tell me about a time you had a bad experience with other content creators.

I have not had any bad experiences from the few times I was able to collab.

4. What is difficult about starting a collab?

Trying to get days/time/game to sync up.

5. What do you need to know about another creator before working with them?

How are they? How the community is. Are the family friendly? Why are they playing said game I am willing to collab with them.

6. Would you like a central place to connect with people to meet, chat, and schedule collab opportunities?

Yes that would be nice and very helpful.

7. What features would you like to see in a content creator connection app (like messaging, calendar, filtering options, tagging, block feature, etc.)?

Messaging for sure, community guidelines (we dont wont creeps/weirdos) blocking, calenders, reminders. A place for everyone to chat thats NOT just for the collab you are doing.

8. What would make you want to make a profile on our app?

Possible collabs, new friends, new opportunities.

9. What would keep you from wanting to make a profile on our app?

If i gets a bad rep, noticing creeps/people ruining it for others. It doesnt do what I thought it would do.

10. Do you have any ideas/suggestions for us? (We love feedback!)

Not at the moment but if I do I will be sure to add later on
