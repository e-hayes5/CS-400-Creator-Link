Interview 4

Date: 09/23/2026

Interviewer: Liz Olin

Format: Virtual

Interview notes

1. How do you feel currently about finding other content creators to collab with?

I think mostly I feel uncomfortable trying to find or approach other creators to collab with. I have my close friends online that I collab with regularly but other than those 2 or 3 people I really dont feel comfortable seeking others out.

2. What problem would you like to solve as related to collaborating?

I think collaborating sometimes brings a stigma especially if you're reaching out to slightly bigger creators. That would be something that I would love to see solved. I know its naive but it would be amazing to feel that people are just willing to help others grow alongside them and not just get a leg up.

3. Tell me about a time you had a bad experience with other content creators.

Luckily i can honestly say I've not had many if any bad experiences with other creators. I have had times where I've co-streamed with people and maybe our chats just didn't vibe well together. Thankfully that was a good friend of mine and a simple conversation afterwards solved that problem for the next time...we just wouldn't do shared chat again.

4. What is difficult about starting a collab?

Linking back to my previous answer, i think that would be one of the biggest difficulties about starting a collab. Without really "vetting" the other creator its so hard to know if your communities will vibe well together or if your sense of humours and energy levels match.

5. What do you need to know about another creator before working with them?

I think I kind of answered this one in my previous answer too. But on top of having an idea about sense of humour and community vibe, I think its important to know the other creators boundaries. I have quite a dark sense of humour and I'm incredibly sarcastic (from the UK) and I do often worry that I come across as being a bit of a tool when i'm just making a joke.

6. Would you like a central place to connect with people to meet, chat, and schedule collab opportunities?

YES!! I think that would be incredible!

7. What features would you like to see in a content creator connection app (like messaging, calendar, filtering options, tagging, block feature, etc.)?

I would love to see all of those options. If it was discord-esq there could be roles for people in certain timezones or those willing to collab with people in any timezone. Definitely a report/block feature for admins to see potential problem creators and take action. Ways of easily searching for creators via content genre, ARPG's, looter shooters, FPS, Just chatting etc. Also as a big charity stream fan, having a section for charity collabs would be amazing too!

8. What would make you want to make a profile on our app?

I think just seeing a well put together app, that's gone through testing and works reliably and offers some solid options for finding creators, that would make me want to join up! I think too many apps/programmes try and do too much and be too fancy and end up buggy messes haha.

9. What would keep you from wanting to make a profile on our app?

I think the only thing really that would put me off signing up to an app like that would be if I saw horror stories of people being bullied by other creators or maybe being overlooked or left out for being a smaller creator. As somebody who averages 25-30 ccv on Twitch, which I know isn't huge but it also isn't bad at all...If i thought myself or my community would be at risk in any way then that would put me off for sure.

10. Do you have any ideas/suggestions for us? (We love feedback!)

I think my feedback/ideas are scattered around in these answers. I love the idea and I would love to be involved if any opportunities arise for testing. As a smaller creator i'd love a localised place where I can meet likeminded people and grow as a creator alongside others!
