Interview 3

Date: 09/23/2026

Interviewer: Liz Olin

Format: Virtual

Interview notes

1. How do you feel currently about finding other content creators to collab with?

Currently it takes effort. As somebody who does content part time and as a hobby, a lot of my friends in the space stream in the morning. Them being full time means when I am at my full time job, they are streaming. So when they want to collab, I can't. This means I need to do all of the legwork to get everyone else together most of the time. Also, many of the creators I know are pulled in a lot of directions and fly by the seat of their pants. That means when I reach out it is sometimes missed or lost in the shuffle requiring me to ask considerably early than I even want to plan. Sometimes a month out.

2. What problem would you like to solve as related to collaborating?

I don't want to solve any problems when it comes to collaborating? It would be nice as a smaller streamer if I was asked more to collaborate but I have been in this space long enough to know everything falls on you. You either put in the work or you don't.

3. Tell me about a time you had a bad experience with other content creators.

I have had a few bad experiences with other content creators. When collaborating specifically I know of one that stands out. They were part of a vetted discord (company creator discord) and a new game had dropped directly related to said discord. I placed an open invite to creators in the discord to collab and do 2hr sessions. I figured two hours was enough to get to know people but not be stuck if it went sideways and allow enough of the creators from this discord the ability to get to know me as I had 20+ timeslots available. Each two hour slot had two spaces as withe game involved trios. This creator during our session dropped some borderline racist statements. Our 3rd was somebody I had never played with either but I knew their content and had wanted to play with them for years prior. We both professionally navigated the statement and you could tell the rest of the session was awkward. I am glad it was only a two hour session but I avoid that creator to this day and it definitely made future collaborations with the creator I liked to be much more difficult.

4. What is difficult about starting a collab?

I dont think there is anything inherently difficult about starting a collab, but I excel at networking and I've branded myself as somebody who is easy to work with. For others I would imagine scheduling, finding the right people, vetting those people, having to punch up sometimes and reach out to larger creators who are very guarded, and the list goes on. Being in this space for 7+ years and working in charity where collaboration is almost a given, I find it easier than most would.

5. What do you need to know about another creator before working with them?

Typically I need to know a lot about them. The genres they play come first to see if we have any crossover in content or a way to bridge our content. Ex: [Creator A] doesnt play Destiny but enjoys FPS content on a semi competitive level. So we have a shared interest. The second thing I look at is what content do they make. Somebody like [Creator B] plays Destiny and FPS games but plays competitively and content revolves around skill only. So going deeper into how they make their content matters because I should also be a fit for them. If I decide to collab with [Creator B], I know I am relying on playing very hard and competing where if I play with [Creator A] its expected to have more fun. Next I want to know who they are as a person. This is one of the hardest and the one many of us get burned on and have stories about. A lot of content creators hide who they truly are and its hard to tell who is genuinely the same person you see on screen. If I have met them at a convention (shoutout GCX Event and StJudePlayLive Summit) I have more information about them. I prefer to work with people I have met over those I have not.

6. Would you like a central place to connect with people to meet, chat, and schedule collab opportunities?

I don't know that a place like that is needed, for me. I network very well and I am open to more tools for networking but I have many and is one more going to replace any of the others? All of the others are multipurpose such as Discord, Twitter, Twitch chat. and real life interactions. It would need to replace something or have a multipurpose or have others reaching out to me taking away the effort I have to put in on my end.

7. What features would you like to see in a content creator connection app (like messaging, calendar, filtering options, tagging, block feature, etc.)?

I am unsure what features I would want to see.

8. What would make you want to make a profile on our app?

If the app brings value to what I do, I would join. If the app has enough people already invested, regardless of the value it brings to me, I would join simply for others to find me to collab.

9. What would keep you from wanting to make a profile on our app?

Fees. Hurdles.

10. Do you have any ideas/suggestions for us? (We love feedback!)

No suggestions or feedback at this time. ❤️
