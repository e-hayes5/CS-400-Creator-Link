Interview 7

Date: 09/22/2026

Interviewer: Liz Olin

Format: Virtual

Interview notes

1. How do you feel currently about finding other content creators to collab with?

I personally stick to my circles pretty tightly. I’ve been proven more times than not that there are some s***ty people in this space. I don’t reach out because of fear. Can we have a review feature??? Like 5 stars ([Creator] big creators will just bail. Keep delaying it or not showing up) I’d like to know if this person I want to collab with is super flakey. Are the viewers reliable? Good to chat with? Overly excited?

2. What problem would you like to solve as related to collaborating?

Scheduling always comes up. If there was a way to make it clear and lock it down that would be helpful. Lets say there were 10 of the biggest podcasts in the world and they were looking to find people to interview, you could put your hat in the ring. MrBeast video wants 100 creators and you could put your hat in the ring. Scheduling would be number 1. Tbh I don’t collab as much as others. I shut down when I’m in a group. I become more quiet when I collab. Finding the people who vibe with me. Tags likes to roast eachother/trolls.

3. Tell me about a time you had a bad experience with other content creators.

I joined a podcast episode and it felt exploitative. They were trying to use me for views. They had a big following so I thought they were chill and they weren’t.

4. What is difficult about starting a collab?

The reach-out process. They don’t want to be rejected. Feels like a cold-call sometimes. Might be really appealing (the app). Outreach and being comfy enough to make a collab happen.

5. What do you need to know about another creator before working with them?

If they’re racist and crazy lol. Are they controversial? Is there controversy behind them? Really knowing who I’m getting into bed with. If it’s a larger collab/sponsored thing, are they family friendly, are they not. Sponsored content type of players. Are they good at the game and also good for content? From the CC side of things, if there was a way—wow: 3 people who log in at launch and want to grind for 6 hours.

6. Would you like a central place to connect with people to meet, chat, and schedule collab opportunities?

I don’t think I’m the target audience. I’m not super keen on it, but that doesn’t mean it’s a bad thing. I’m antisocial a little bit.

7. What features would you like to see in a content creator connection app (like messaging, calendar, filtering options, tagging, block feature, etc.)?

Profile to look up what games they play. Tap into the API somehow to see what games they play. Rating to see that they’re not weirdos. Scheduling. Messaging or chat system. notifications

8. What would make you want to make a profile on our app?

Other people hyping it. I’m not a first-mover in stuff like this. But if it’s the hot new social media that people are enjoying then yes. If it’s a direct recommendation from a friend then that goes a lot further.

9. What would keep you from wanting to make a profile on our app?

My own reservations of how much I would use it. If it’s a good product, there’s no reason not to use it. Do I see as much value in it as other people? I’m a bit of a loner with it.

10. Do you have any ideas/suggestions for us? (We love feedback!)

Banning people. Needs to be linked to steam or twitch api. Emails or phone numbers or something so people can’t ban evade and troll. Do a lot of market research before putting a bunch of money into it. [Creator]’s friend is creating a thing called hotdrop: it’s an LFG and keeps up with what your community is doing. A new way to hang out with your community. Loyalty badges. Link up all your content to keep your audience in the loop.
