Interview 1

Date: 09/24/2026

Interviewer: Aidan Cissell

Format: Virtual

Interview notes

1. How do you feel currently about finding other content creators to collab with?

Right now it’s very organic through the game communities I’m part of: DayZ specifically. Otherwise, I try to make connections, hang out in chats, and allow it to come up organically. Definitely more nuanced than direct “hey, let's collab” as soon as I meet someone.

2. What problem would you like to solve as related to collaborating?

My biggest hurdles are finding times to coordinate, especially across time zones (though that’s really more of an issue for the people involved). Another is setting expectations for how a collab would work, including consolidating chat / cross-promotion of content.

3. Tell me about a time you had a bad experience with other content creators.

Luckily, I haven’t had too many “truly” bad experiences, but I think if there’s a way to quantify how well one could “gel” with another content creator, it would probably help a lot. I’ve had instances where our energy levels or comedic styles didn’t sync up, which caused some awkward moments.

4. What is difficult about starting a collab?

I think it’s a lot like dating: how many “dates,” or how much time you spend in each other's communities, before you can ask them to collab without it seeming self-serving. That “unknown” of how long it’s appropriate to wait is always a hurdle, ESPECIALLY if there’s a distinct possibility they also want to collab but have the same worry.

5. What do you need to know about another creator before working with them?

Their style, how they involve their community, timezone/stream time preference/game preference

6. Would you like a central place to connect with people to meet, chat, and schedule collab opportunities?

God yes, please.

7. What features would you like to see in a content creator connection app (like messaging, calendar, filtering options, tagging, block feature, etc.)?

Messaging, syncing calendar, filtering, block feature, some sort of collaboration timeline (hey, I'm likely to collab in X amount of time)

Sort of like LinkedIn: instead of open to work, it's open to collab

8. What would make you want to make a profile on our app?

Ability to connect with like-minded and VETTED creators who abide by a certain decorum (no grifters please).

9. What would keep you from wanting to make a profile on our app?

Heavy A.I. generated content in terms of creative endeavours like art or writing.

10. Do you have any ideas/suggestions for us? (We love feedback!)

Definitely a way to ease the tension and uncertainty about when it’s okay to request a collab.
