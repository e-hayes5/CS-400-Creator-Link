Interview 5

Date: 09/25/2026

Interviewer: Bernie Jobel Diaz

Format: Virtual

Interview notes

1. How do you feel currently about finding other content creators to collab with?

He feels okay about finding collaborators through social media but thinks having a platform specifically made to help creators collaborate would make it easier.

2. What problem would you like to solve as related to collaborating?

His biggest concern is communication and being able to use an app to verify other creators.

3. Tell me about a time you had a bad experience with other content creators.

He said that creators sometimes message him asking for advice and may steal his ideas. He also mentioned that some creators may use him mainly to get more views.

4. What is difficult about starting a collab?

He said that trust is a major concern when working with other creators.

5. What do you need to know about another creator before working with them?

He would like some type of verification or creator background, like a résumé, before collaborating. He also mentioned getting a lot of emails from the same people or receiving spam, so verification would help him know who he is communicating with.

6. Would you like a central place to connect with people to meet, chat, and schedule collab opportunities?

Yes. He would like to have one place where creators can connect, communicate, and schedule collaborations.

7. What features would you like to see in a content creator connection app (like messaging, calendar, filtering options, tagging, block feature, etc.)?

He would like all of the features mentioned, including messaging, a calendar, filtering options, tagging, and a block feature.

8. What would make you want to make a profile on our app?

Trust would be important to him. He also liked the idea of being able to send a request before someone can message him, similar to Discord.

9. What would keep you from wanting to make a profile on our app?

He said it would depend on how personal information is handled. He would want his personal information to stay protected and not be shared without permission.

10. Do you have any ideas/suggestions for us? (We love feedback!)

He suggested making it easier for users to collaborate and adding useful features from other social and communication apps. He also suggested having both public and private messaging options.
