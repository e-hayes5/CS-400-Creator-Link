Interview 6

Date: 09/22/2026

Interviewer: Liz Olin

Format: Virtual

Interview notes

1. How do you feel currently about finding other content creators to collab with?

Nightmare to find people to collab with. 1) hard schedule-wise because I’m very close to not needing to stream full-time anymore. Core problem: inconsistent streams. I hate being like opp sorry I can’t and I have to take a meeting. Being able to use it as an LFG situation. This evening from 5-9 I’m going to be able to playing one game, and they can hop in would be wonderful. Would give me a layer of social comfort because everyone is looking for collaboration. Very exhausting social load.

Like Club.com

Or Hotdrop: they’re building something to be content creator first

This could be like Microsoft acquiring Beam and turning it into mixer

Could be self-sustaining and very compelling for people

2. What problem would you like to solve as related to collaborating?

Scheduling. Social aspect. Especially since I’m less involved as I used to be. Put the flag out and allow people make appointments with me. Put something out and had to take it down and communicate to them (all 5 people in a raid) at the same time that I can’t be there. Solves a problem for them trying to find a fill. Calendar feature and/or shareable weblink. Community day with wow forever pick your time slot for 6 people and the next time slot could be 6 new people. Trials carries as an example.

3. Tell me about a time you had a bad experience with other content creators.

When people won’t shut the f*** up. If I could flag somebody as a yapper. Don’t mute their mic on appropriate times. Open-micing that s*** is exhausting. Streamer etiquette. How does that not also get weaponized. I mute my mic #hasamutebutton. I run my stream like this, etc. Add a call and video chat function to vibe so it’s not a first date situation

4. What is difficult about starting a collab?

Scheduling. Comms (back and forth of actually setting it up).

5. What do you need to know about another creator before working with them?

Are they family friendly or not is a huge one. What console do they play the game on? Gamertag so that can get set up ahead of time. possibly a privately shared profile information How do you deal with a troll? I’m going to make an example of them and laugh about them. Have had bad experiences with people who have different experiences with moderation. Obviously if I say something offensive, that’s on me. (used the word “insane” and didn’t like that). how do we tackle this issue??

6. Would you like a central place to connect with people to meet, chat, and schedule collab opportunities?

Yes

7. What features would you like to see in a content creator connection app (like messaging, calendar, filtering options, tagging, block feature, etc.)?

Messaging, scheduling, automatic communication, notifications. Possibly managed by and/or looking to collab on a sponsored thing. During this period of time I’ll have a sponsored segment and/or be front page.

8. What would make you want to make a profile on our app?

Everything we talked about. Just the raw ability to schedule something would get me to sign up.

9. What would keep you from wanting to make a profile on our app?

Data exposure. If it wasn’t secure, If It got hacked.

10. Do you have any ideas/suggestions for us? (We love feedback!)

Collaboration type: if I was organizing a raid in destiny what type of character I’m bringing. Playstyle. What kind of gamer are you? Do you wait or run ahead? #polite.
