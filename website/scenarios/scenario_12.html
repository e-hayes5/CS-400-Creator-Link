Rebecca's scenario: Co-creating a wellness challenge

Persona: Rebecca

Rebecca now feels confident enough to lead her first collab. She wants to run a 7-day "Morning Reset" challenge with two other wellness creators, each covering a different area: movement, nutrition and mindfulness. Planning by Instagram DM would be hard to keep track of.

Rebecca creates a collab project on KreaterLink and invites Jasmine and Leah, a meditation creator she met through the community space. Before inviting Leah, she checks Leah's verified credentials and reviews from past collab partners. The project has a shared posting calendar, a task list showing who films and edits each day's video, and a shared folder for the challenge's template, fonts and colors.

KreaterLink reminds each creator the day before their video is due. Because all three creators use the same template, the series looks consistent across all three accounts. Rebecca gains 400 followers by the end of the week.
