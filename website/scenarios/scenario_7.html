Marcus's scenario: Posting a last-minute LFG window

Persona: Marcus

On Tuesday afternoon, one of Marcus's meetings is canceled and he suddenly has a free evening. He would like to stream Destiny 2 with other creators, but asking people individually at short notice feels like a heavy social load, and most will already have plans.

Marcus opens KreaterLink and posts an LFG (looking-for-group) window: Destiny 2 on PlayStation, 5 to 9 p.m. Mountain, three open spots, family-friendly content only. Creators who follow him or who list Destiny 2 on their profile get a notification. Because everyone who answers an LFG post is looking to play, Marcus doesn't worry about being a bother.

Within an hour, three creators join the window. Each has shared their gamertag with the group through a private profile field, so Marcus can send fireteam invites before going live. He also checks each joiner's playstyle and etiquette tags, such as "mutes when not talking," so he knows what to expect from the session.
