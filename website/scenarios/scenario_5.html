Derek's scenario: Getting answers from busy creators

Persona: Derek

Derek is organizing a six-creator Destiny raid night for a charity campaign a month away. Busy creators often miss his Twitter DMs and Discord pings, so he usually has to follow up several times and still doesn't know who is in.

Derek creates the event on KreaterLink and sends collab invites to the six creators he has in mind. Each invite appears in a dedicated collab inbox, separate from general messages, with the date, time, game and purpose shown up front. Derek can see whether each invite has been seen, accepted or declined.

Two creators accept on the first day. For the others, KreaterLink sends an automatic reminder after three days, so Derek doesn't have to chase them himself. One creator declines, and Derek invites a replacement from his saved list. A day before the raid, KreaterLink reminds everyone of the start time in their own time zone.
