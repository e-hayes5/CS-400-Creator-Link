Maya's scenario: Organizing a charity collab

Persona: Maya

Maya wants to run a 12-hour charity stream for a children's hospital in November, with several co-streamers covering different blocks. Last year she posted about it on Twitter and the replies were scattered across DMs, quote tweets and Discord. Several people dropped out without telling her.

This year Maya opens KreaterLink's charity collabs section and creates an event. She enters the cause, the date, six two-hour slots and the games she would like featured. Creators who have said they are interested in charity events get a notification.

Over the next week, eight creators apply. Maya reviews their profiles, accepts five and assigns each one a slot on the event calendar. KreaterLink creates a group chat for the event, and each creator exports their slot to Google Calendar. KreaterLink sends everyone a reminder the week before and again on the day. When one creator has to drop out, Maya reopens that slot and a creator who applied earlier claims it.
