Marcus's scenario: Running a community day with bookable slots

Persona: Marcus

Marcus wants to run a World of Warcraft community day where viewers and smaller creators can play with him. In the past he collected names in Discord and tried to fit them into groups by hand, which took most of a day and left some people out.

Marcus creates a community day event on KreaterLink with eight one-hour slots. Each slot has room for six players, and every slot gets a new group. KreaterLink generates a shareable web link, which Marcus posts on Twitter and in his Discord. People who open the link pick a slot without needing to message him.

As slots fill, Marcus sees the full roster on his calendar. Each participant receives a confirmation and a reminder an hour before their slot. On the day, Marcus simply works through the schedule, and nobody is left wondering whether they are in.
