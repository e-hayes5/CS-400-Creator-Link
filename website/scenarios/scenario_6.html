Derek's scenario: Vetting a new collab partner

Persona: Derek

Derek once ran an open collab where a creator he didn't know made borderline racist comments on stream, which hurt his relationship with another creator in the session. Since then he prefers to work with people he has met in person at events, but that limits who he can play with.

When a creator named Priya claims one of his open slots, Derek opens her KreaterLink profile before confirming. He sees that her Twitch account is verified and that she plays FPS games semi-competitively but focuses on fun, which fits his style. Her profile shows she is family friendly. He also reads reviews from creators she has collabed with on KreaterLink, and he sees that two creators he trusts are mutual connections, one of whom tagged her as "met at GCX."

Satisfied, Derek confirms the slot. He adds a short note to the session with his ground rules: keep it family friendly and mute when not talking.
