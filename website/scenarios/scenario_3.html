Maya's scenario: Reporting a creator who crossed a line

Persona: Maya

Maya does her first collab with Dan, a creator she met on KreaterLink. During the stream, Dan repeatedly makes jokes at the expense of one of Maya's viewers, and his chat spams hers. Maya ends the stream early. She feels her community was put at risk but doesn't want to start public drama on Twitter.

After the stream, Maya opens Dan's KreaterLink profile and selects Report. She chooses "harassment of my community" as the reason, writes a short description and adds a timestamp from her VOD. She then blocks Dan, so he can no longer message her, see her availability or appear in her search results.

KreaterLink confirms that it received the report, and a moderator reviews it. A few days later Maya is notified that action has been taken. Knowing that problem creators are dealt with makes her comfortable using the app again.
