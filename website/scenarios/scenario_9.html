Marcus's scenario: Canceling a session without letting people down

Persona: Marcus

Marcus has a Destiny raid scheduled for Thursday night with five other players. On Thursday morning a business meeting is moved to that evening and he can no longer play. Previously, he had to message each person separately and apologize, and the rest of the group was left without a sixth player.

Marcus opens the raid on KreaterLink and selects "I can't make it." He chooses whether to reschedule or let the group continue without him. He decides to open his spot to other players. KreaterLink automatically notifies all five players and posts the open spot to creators looking for a raid that night.

Another creator fills Marcus's spot within two hours, so the raid goes ahead. Marcus also suggests two alternative times for a future raid, and the group votes on one in the event chat.
