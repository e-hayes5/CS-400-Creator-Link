Maya's scenario: Finding creators who match her vibe

Persona: Maya

Maya has streamed for two years and wants to grow by collabing with creators outside her small group of friends. She dislikes cold-messaging strangers on Twitter or Discord, because she has no way of telling whether their community will mesh with hers or whether her sarcastic humor will land. A co-stream where the two chats didn't get along has made her more cautious.

Maya creates a KreaterLink profile. She lists her genres (ARPGs and looter shooters), her UK time zone, her usual stream times and tags such as "sarcastic" and "dark humor." She adds a short note about her boundaries. She then searches for looter shooter creators in European time zones with a similar audience size and the "sarcastic" tag.

She opens three profiles and reads each creator's content style, community description and boundaries, then watches a linked clip. One creator, Leo, looks like a strong match. Because everyone on KreaterLink is there to collab, reaching out feels expected rather than awkward. Maya sends Leo a short message introducing herself and suggests a casual voice call before they plan a stream.
