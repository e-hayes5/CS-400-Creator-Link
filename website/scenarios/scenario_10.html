Rebecca's scenario: Finding a creator to help with editing

Persona: Rebecca

Rebecca spends most of her Sunday editing one yoga video, and she's frustrated with how it turns out. She has tried YouTube tutorials, but they are long and aimed at professional editors. She would rather learn from another wellness creator who understands the style of content she makes.

Rebecca opens KreaterLink and searches for creators in the Health & Wellness niche who list "video editing" under the skills they can help with. She finds Jasmine, a registered dietitian with 40,000 followers whose videos have a clean, consistent look. Jasmine's profile shows that her credentials have been verified and that she is open to skill swaps. When Rebecca shows interest in her and Jasmine does the same, KreaterLink matches them and notifies Rebecca of the new match.

Rebecca sends Jasmine a skill-swap request. In exchange for an hour-long editing session, she offers to film a short yoga segment for one of Jasmine's videos. Jasmine accepts, and they pick a time for a video call using KreaterLink's calendar, which syncs to both of their Google Calendars.
