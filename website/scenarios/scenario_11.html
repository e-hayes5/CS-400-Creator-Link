Rebecca's scenario: Getting feedback on her content style

Persona: Rebecca

After her session with Jasmine, Rebecca edits faster, but she still isn't sure her videos look like they belong to one brand. Asking friends hasn't helped, because they aren't creators and just say everything looks great.

Rebecca opens the Health & Wellness community space on KreaterLink and creates a feedback request. She uploads a draft reel and three thumbnail options, and asks for feedback on color, fonts and her opening hook. Only KreaterLink members in the wellness community can see the post.

Within a day, four creators leave comments. Some are tied to specific moments in the video, such as "the text at 0:04 is hard to read." Two creators suggest a warmer color filter, and one shares the font pairing she uses. Rebecca saves the suggestions into a simple style guide on her profile and uses it for every post that week.
