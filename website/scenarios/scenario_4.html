Derek's scenario: Posting open collab slots around his day job

Persona: Derek

A new trio-based shooter launches on Friday and Derek wants to play it with as many creators as possible during launch week. Most of his creator friends stream full time in the mornings, while he is at work. In the past he messaged people one by one on Discord and juggled replies in a spreadsheet, which took hours.

This time Derek opens KreaterLink and creates an open collab post for the game. He marks his availability as weeknights from 7 to 11 p.m. Eastern and Saturday afternoon. KreaterLink splits that time into two-hour sessions, each with two open spots, and he sets the post to "FPS creators, any size."

Creators who play the game see the post in their feed and claim the slots that suit them. Each claim appears on Derek's KreaterLink calendar, which syncs to his Google Calendar, and he can see who is joining each session. By Thursday, 14 of his 20 spots are filled and Derek hasn't had to send a single message to make it happen.
