Persona 3: Marcus

Marcus, an established creator with an unpredictable schedule

Marcus is 38 and lives in Denver, Colorado. He has streamed full time for several years and runs Destiny raids, "community days" and the occasional sponsored segment for a large, family-friendly community. He is moving toward a business role that will mean he no longer streams full time.

Marcus's schedule is now inconsistent because meetings come up at short notice. He finds the social load of setting up collabs exhausting, and he hates canceling on groups of people one by one. He has strong views on streamer etiquette: he mutes his mic when appropriate and expects partners to do the same.

Marcus has a master's degree in education and taught before becoming a creator.

Marcus would sign up for scheduling alone. He wants to post looking-for-group (LFG) windows, let people book time slots, and notify everyone automatically if plans change. Before a collab he wants to see a creator's platform, gamertag, playstyle and whether they are family friendly. He is concerned about the security of any profile data he shares.

Scenarios

  • Posting a last-minute LFG window
  • Running a community day with bookable slots
  • Canceling a session without letting people down
