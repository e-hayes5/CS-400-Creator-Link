Persona 4: Rebecca

Rebecca, a beginning health and wellness creator

Rebecca is 26 and lives in Raleigh, North Carolina. Six months ago she started posting health and wellness content on Instagram and TikTok, including beginner yoga flows, simple meal prep and mental wellness tips. She has about 1,200 followers and posts three times a week.

Rebecca works full time as a physical therapy assistant and films her content on evenings and weekends. Editing a single 60-second video in CapCut can take her three hours, and her posts don't have a consistent look. Her lighting, fonts and colors change from video to video. She knows her content would grow faster if it looked more polished, but she doesn't know where to start.

Rebecca has a bachelor's degree in kinesiology and is a certified yoga instructor. She has no training in video editing, photography or design.

Rebecca wants to connect with other health and wellness creators who can help her with editing and styling her content. She needs to search by niche and by the skills other creators offer, and she wants to swap skills, since she can offer yoga and fitness expertise in return. Because wellness content can spread misinformation, she also wants to see a creator's credentials before working with them.

Scenarios

  • Finding a creator to help with editing
  • Getting feedback on her content style
  • Co-creating a wellness challenge
