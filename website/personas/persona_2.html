Derek

A part-time streamer with a day job

Derek is 31 and lives in Columbus, Ohio. He has been creating content for over seven years as a hobby, streaming Destiny and other FPS games on weeknight evenings and weekends. He is well networked, attends events such as GCX and St. Jude PLAY LIVE, and has built a reputation as someone who is easy to work with.

Derek works full time as an IT support analyst, so he is at work when most of his full-time creator friends are live. As a result, he usually does the legwork of organizing group collabs himself. His messages often get lost in other creators' busy feeds, so he sometimes has to start planning a month in advance. Derek also has a bachelor's degree in information technology. He has organized charity streams and multi-creator events, and he is confident with scheduling tools. He doesn't feel like he needs another networking app. He will only join if it saves him effort over Discord and Twitter, or if enough creators are already on it that others come to him. He would leave over fees or sign-up hurdles. He would value open time slots that other creators can claim, and reminders so plans don't get lost.
